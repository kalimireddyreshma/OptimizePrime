# ContractMind AI project story

## Before

Contract reviewers repeatedly searched older contracts and emails to answer the same question: what did our company decide about a similar clause before?

## During

ContractMind AI reads the current contract, identifies the clause category, recalls related historical decisions from the organization, and compares the current clause with previously approved patterns.

## After

The reviewer receives a recommendation backed by evidence and confidence. The organization learns from each accepted or modified human decision, which makes future reviews more consistent, faster, and aligned with prior practice.
