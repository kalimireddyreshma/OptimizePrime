# ContractMind AI demo video script

Problem

A legal team reviews repeated vendor clauses. The same liability and payment issues come up again and again. The team struggles to remember what the company has historically accepted.

ContractMind solution

ContractMind AI reviews the clause and asks a stronger question: what has our organization already decided for clauses like this?

Hindsight memory

The app is built around Hindsight-style persistent memory. It retains approved decisions, recalls relevant precedent, and reflects on repeated patterns over time.

Human decision

The recommendation is shown with clear evidence. A reviewer can accept, modify, reject, or ignore it. The decision becomes part of the next teaching loop.

Learning

As more decisions are approved, confidence increases and the preference becomes clearer. The application explains the evidence behind the recommendation instead of pretending the model knows the organization by default.

Future recommendation

On a new contract, the system recalls earlier decisions, recommends the standard position, and shows why it is consistent with prior organizational behavior.
