ContractMind AI is a practical legal-tech prototype built for HackWithHyderabad 3.0. It combines AI contract review with organizational memory using Hindsight so the system remembers how a company actually negotiates — not just what a clause means.

The goal is simple: reduce repetitive contract review, preserve institutional knowledge, and make contractual decisions more consistent over time.

A reviewer sees a clause, the system recalls similar historical decisions, explains the evidence, and recommends a position grounded in prior human-approved choices. The reviewer still decides — accept, modify, reject, or ignore — and each decision feeds the next cycle of learning.

This is not generic legal AI. It is an organization-aware memory system for deal terms and contract negotiation patterns.
